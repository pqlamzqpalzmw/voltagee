GAME PROTECTION / DMCA

I have also recently started helping with the DMCA process for games. I begin by researching games on Roblox and looking for games that may have names or branding similar to an existing project. From there, I review the game and look for potential unauthorized use of assets or other material. I organize the information I find so that the appropriate rights holder can review the situation and decide what action to take.
